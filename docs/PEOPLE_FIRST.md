# People-First framing (draft)

**Named community:** people waiting on a diagnosis for suspected PCOS or
endometriosis — navigating training and daily energy while clinical answers
are delayed.

**Privacy design (built):** no name, email, or phone; a one-time 10-character
login code; age is the only personal field; audio and raw transcripts are not
stored.

**Impact metric (draft, not verified):** share of check-ins completed in under
60 seconds across five real runs, recorded in `docs/VERIFIED.md` after the
owner confirms on a real device.

**Real quote:** still required from a real person in this community. Do not
invent a quote for the pitch or submission.
